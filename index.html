§ 02

Research

Living systems are dynamic, spatially organized, and continuously evolving.

I develop bioelectronic, material, and AI technologies that progressively expand how we interface with them — from chronic sensing inside intact tissues, to materials written into specified cells, to agents that interpret data and run experiments.

I

Cyborg tissue–bioelectronic hybrids for chronic, multimodal sensing and actuation

I embed stretchable mesh nanoelectronics into organoids as they develop, so sensors and stimulators grow seamlessly into 3D tissue. In human pancreatic organoids, this enables months-long, single-cell recordings of α and β cells, links their electrical signatures to transcriptional identity through in situ electro-sequencing, and lets embedded stimulators enhance glucose-dependent responses.

II

Programmable material interfaces for spatially precise biological modulation

I write function directly into living cells. Optogenetic polymerization uses genetically encoded photosensitizers and light to deposit conductive or insulating polymers on specified cells, stepwise lowering or raising neuronal excitability with genetic, spatial, and temporal precision.

III

Agentic AI for interpreting, designing, and executing biological workflows

I build AI agents that interpret biology and act on it. STAgent turns weeks of expert spatial transcriptomics analysis into minutes; Agentic Lab carries agents into the physical lab, designing protocols, guiding hands-on work through augmented reality, and refining experiments from what it observes.
