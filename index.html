About

Hi, I am Wenbo, currently a Postdoctoral Associate at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, working with Professor Xiao Wang. I received my PhD in Engineering Sciences from Harvard University, where I was advised by Professor Jia Liu at Harvard SEAS.

My research focuses on the intersection of AI and biomedicine, where I build agentic AI systems, biointegrated electronics, and programmable material interfaces to sense, interpret, and adaptively modulate biological systems across molecular, cellular, tissue, and clinical scales.

I earned my bachelor's degree in chemistry from Zhejiang University, where I was in the Qiushi Honors Program of Chu Kochen Honors College. During my undergraduate studies, I explored supramolecular chemistry under the guidance of Professor Feihe Huang in Zhejiang University and Professor Peter J. Stang at the University of Utah.

I also worked as a visiting student during pandemic at Westlake University with Professor Yi Sun, studying the circuit, molecular and functional basis of Drosophila's visual system.

II

Cyborg tissue–bioelectronic hybrids for chronic, multimodal sensing and actuation

I build cyborg tissue platforms that integrate stretchable mesh nanoelectronics into developing 3D tissues and organoids, enabling long-term, tissue-wide, single-cell-resolution electrophysiology and seamless integration with spatial transcriptomics. These systems link function to cell identity, gene expression, and tissue microenvironment — and move from passive sensing toward adaptive modulation of biological systems.
